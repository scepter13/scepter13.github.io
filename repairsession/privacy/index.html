Provided by individual developer SEO DAEGYU (리팩토리 / Refactory) — the "Developer".

At a glance

  • Personal data collected: none. There are no accounts or sign-in, the Developer runs no server, and the App sends no data over the internet.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases.

1. Data stored only on your device

To let you continue your game, the following is stored only in the App's storage on your device: your current chapter, in-game trust value, the player name you choose to enter (optional), play statistics, the endings you have seen, and your language, sound and reduce-flashing settings. It never leaves your device and the Developer cannot see it. It is deleted when you erase it in the App's settings or delete the App.

2. Location permission

At one point in the story the App may ask for iOS "While Using the App" location permission. If you allow it, the App reads an approximate (kilometer-level) location once, on your device, and discards it immediately. The location is not shown, not stored, and not sent to the Developer or anyone else. If you decline, the game continues normally. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

3. Children

The App contains psychological horror and is not directed at children. The Developer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
